Creatives for Collectives

[RECORD: DES-01]

Local Threads Collective was born in the Mumbai suburbs in 2023 with a singular focus: resisting urban loneliness by bringing people together.

Through small-scale gatherings of 10 to 15 people, the community explores themes ranging from sustainability and personal finance to quiet "Offline Hours" and shared leisure.

My work with the collective involves translating that mission into visual assets. Collaborating with other facilitators and members, I design the creatives that populate our digital platforms and our physical meetups. I am also taking up a new project with the aim to redesign the community website. I am currently in the early UX Research phase for this project.

The work documented in this record represents the visual identity of those gatherings and a design practice rooted in community building, artistic expression, and meaningful social discussion.

05 / Website Redesign (Ongoing)

Currently executing the foundational UX research phase for the Local Threads Collective website redesign. Before rebuilding the architecture, I am conducting comparative analyses of parallel collectives and gathering qualitative insights from our community members. This data maps out current friction points and defines the structural needs for the new website.
