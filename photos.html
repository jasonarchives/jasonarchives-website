Photos.

[RECORDS : 2018–2024]
My interest in photos began with old family albums, viewing them not just as memories, but as tangible & meaningful artifacts of the past. Today, the camera is my tool to force a pause. It is my deliberate practice of slowing down to observe the quiet architecture of everyday life across places and alongside people. This archive is a visual ledger of those observations, ranging from intimate client portraits to long-exposures of the Aurora Borealis.
I post some of my work on Unsplash ↗, so feel free to download and use my photos from there!
