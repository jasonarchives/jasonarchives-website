Living Empirically

For me, living empirically means that design is an act of inquiry, grounded in observation, participation, and reflection. It is not speculation from a distance, but learning through doing, seeing, and being. Human-Centred and Participatory Design aren’t just techniques to me; they are foundational to my worldview.

Qualitative Strengths

I bring a strong foundation in Qualitative UX Research, through an interest in Ethnography and with a love for methods like Interviews, Participant Observations, Contextual Inquiry and Workshops. From post-it notes to collaborative online platforms, I enjoy working with both tangible and digital tools.

Growth

I am currently working on improving my fluency with Quantitative methods and Data-Driven tools to balance my research practice and contribute holistically across projects. I am also in the process of learning to integrate AI tools into my daily workflow to enchance productivity.
