Designs.

[RECORDS : 2022–2025]
Engineering taught me how to study and build complex systems, but it also revealed a glaring blind spot: the humans expected to use them. I shifted my focus to design because I am fundamentally drawn to the ambiguity of human behavior. I don't observe people simply to optimize interfaces; I observe them because humans are the most fascinating, unpredictable variables in any system. This archive is a living document of that curiosity.
